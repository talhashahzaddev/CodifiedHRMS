What is an Event QR Code?

An Event QR Code is a scannable barcode that contains calendar event data (encoded as a vCalendar or iCalendar string). When a user scans the QR code with their smartphone camera, their phone automatically parses the data and prompts them to save the event directly to their native calendar app.

Why Use QR Codes for Events?

Event QR codes bridge the gap between physical marketing and digital calendars. By printing these codes on flyers, posters, wedding invitations, or conference badges, you eliminate the friction of attendees having to manually type out event dates and times. It drastically increases event attendance and engagement.

How to Generate a Calendar QR Code

Fill out your event details including the title, start/end dates, location, and a brief description. As you type, the QR code on the right updates instantly. Once your details are finalized, click "Download PNG" to save the high-resolution QR code for your print or digital marketing materials.
