What is a Timetable Generator?

A Timetable Generator is a visual scheduling tool that lets you organize classes, meetings, or daily routines into a structured weekly grid. It provides an intuitive interface to map out your week from Monday to Friday.

Why Use a Visual Schedule?

For students, teachers, and professionals, a visual grid makes it significantly easier to identify free blocks of time and avoid scheduling conflicts. Unlike complex digital calendar apps, a static timetable provides a persistent, easily readable overview of your recurring weekly commitments.

How to Create Your Timetable

Use the form to add a subject, select a day of the week, choose the start and end times, and pick a color label. Click "Add Class" to place the block onto the grid. Your schedule is saved automatically to your browser. Once your week is fully planned, click "Download PNG" to save an image of your timetable for printing or sharing.
