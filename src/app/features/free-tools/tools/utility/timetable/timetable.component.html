What is a Timetable Generator?

A timetable generator helps you plan and visualize a recurring weekly schedule across fixed time slots. Whether you're organizing a class schedule, shift roster, study plan, or weekly work routine, a timetable gives you an at-a-glance view of all activities across all days and hours.

This tool lets you build your timetable interactively directly in your browser. Click any cell to add a subject, description, and color. Your timetable auto-saves to localStorage every time you make a change, and exports to CSV (for spreadsheets) or PNG (for sharing or printing).
