What is a UPI QR Code?

A UPI QR Code is a quick-response barcode formatted specifically for the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) system used in India. It encodes a merchant or individual's Virtual Payment Address (VPA / UPI ID), payee name, and optionally, a predefined payment amount.

Why Generate Custom UPI QR Codes?

Freelancers, small business owners, and event organizers can use custom UPI QR codes to collect payments instantly. By embedding a specific amount and transaction note directly into the QR code, you eliminate the chance of the payer entering the wrong amount, making the checkout process frictionless.

How to Create a Payment QR Code

Enter your valid UPI ID (e.g., yourname@bank) and your payee name. You can optionally lock in a specific currency amount and add a transaction note. The QR code updates in real-time. Scan it with any UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) to verify it, then download the PNG to print or share online.
