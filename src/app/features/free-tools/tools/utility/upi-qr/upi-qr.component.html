What is a UPI QR Code?

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) is India's real-time interbank payment system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). A UPI QR code is a machine-readable image that encodes the UPI payment URI in the format: upi://pay?pa=UPI_ID&pn=NAME&am=AMOUNT&tn=NOTE&cu=INR.

When a user scans this QR code with any UPI-enabled app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Amazon Pay), the app opens a payment confirmation screen with all fields pre-filled — making payment fast, accurate, and contactless.

UPI QR Code Use Cases

  • Retail & Shops: Print and display at your counter for contactless payments.
  • Freelancers & Consultants: Include in invoices for instant client payment.
  • Events & Fundraisers: Accept donations and registrations via QR scan.
  • Restaurants: Table QR codes for bill settlement without card machines.
  • Online Businesses: Embed in order confirmation emails as a payment alternative.
