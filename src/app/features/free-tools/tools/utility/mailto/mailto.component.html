What is a Mailto Link?

A mailto: link is a standard HTML hyperlink that instructs the browser to open the user's default email client (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird, etc.) and pre-populate a new compose window with your specified fields. It's the simplest way to add a "Contact Us" button to any website without needing a backend server.

Why URL Encoding Matters

Without proper encoding, special characters like spaces, line breaks, ampersands, and question marks will completely break a mailto link. For example, a space must become %20, and a newline becomes %0A. Our generator automatically applies strict URL encoding to the Subject and Body fields so your links work flawlessly across all browsers.

How to Generate a Mailto URL

Enter the recipient email address, subject line, and the body of your message. You can also add CC and BCC recipients. As you type, the tool generates a raw mailto URL and a ready-to-use HTML anchor tag snippet. Copy the HTML snippet and paste it directly into your website's source code.
