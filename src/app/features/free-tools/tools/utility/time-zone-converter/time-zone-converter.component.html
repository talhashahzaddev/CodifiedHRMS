What is a Time Zone Converter?

A Time Zone Converter is a utility that translates the current or scheduled time from one geographic region to another. It visualizes the time offset between different cities, making it easy to schedule global meetings without doing mental math.

Why is Time Zone Conversion Difficult?

Global timekeeping is complicated by varying UTC offsets, regional Daylight Saving Time (DST) rules, and localized calendar changes. Manually calculating times between places like New York, London, and Tokyo can lead to missed meetings. This tool uses the browser's native IANA timezone database to ensure 100% accurate, DST-aware conversions.

How to Compare Global Times

Search for and select a "Base Timezone" (e.g., your local time) and then add up to three "Target Timezones". The tool will generate a visual 24-hour timeline. You can drag the slider to scrub through the hours of the day and see exactly what time it will be across all selected cities simultaneously.
