What is an Online Time Tracker?

An Online Time Tracker is a browser-based stopwatch and logging utility that helps you monitor exactly how much time you spend on specific tasks, projects, or client work. It provides a running timer and allows you to log sessions into an exportable timesheet.

Why Track Your Time?

Accurate time tracking is essential for freelancers billing by the hour, students managing study sessions, and professionals striving to improve their productivity. By keeping a precise log of your activities, you can identify time-sinks, improve your estimates, and ensure you are compensated accurately for your work.

How to Use the Stopwatch & Logger

Enter a task name and click the "Play" button to start the timer. You can pause and resume the timer as needed. When you are finished with the task, click "Stop & Log" to record the session into your local timesheet. You can accumulate multiple sessions and export the entire log as a CSV file for your records.
