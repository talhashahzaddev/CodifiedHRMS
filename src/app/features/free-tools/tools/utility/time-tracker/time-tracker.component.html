Why Use a Browser-Based Time Tracker?

For freelancers, consultants, developers, and students who need to log time without committing to a full SaaS subscription, a browser-based time tracker is the simplest solution. There's nothing to install, no account to create, and no data leaving your device. Open the page, name your task, click Start, and your time is being tracked.

This tracker uses Date.now() for all elapsed time calculations — not interval tick counting — which means it remains accurate even if the browser tab is throttled, the computer goes to sleep, or the system clock advances while you're away. It also persists the active session to localStorage so that even if you accidentally close the tab, reopening the page will resume the timer from the correct start time.
