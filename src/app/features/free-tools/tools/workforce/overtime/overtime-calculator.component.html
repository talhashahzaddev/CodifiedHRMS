What is an Overtime Calculator?

An overtime calculator splits your total weekly hours into standard hours and premium hours, then applies the appropriate pay multipliers to calculate your total gross wages. It allows both employers and employees to accurately predict weekly payroll costs or take-home pay based on actual hours worked.

How to Calculate Overtime

Regular Hours = Min(Total Hours, Standard Limit)
Overtime Hours = Max(0, Total Hours − Standard Limit)
Regular Pay = Regular Hours × Hourly Rate
Overtime Pay = Overtime Hours × Hourly Rate × Overtime Multiplier
Total Gross Pay = Regular Pay + Overtime Pay

Worked Example

Rate: $20/hr. Total Hours: 45. Standard: 40. Multiplier: 1.5x

  • Regular Hours: 40. Overtime Hours: 45 - 40 = 5.
  • Regular Pay = 40 × $20 = $800
  • Overtime Rate = $20 × 1.5 = $30/hr
  • Overtime Pay = 5 × $30 = $150
  • Total Gross Pay = $800 + $150 = $950
