What is a Leave Encashment Calculator?

A leave encashment calculator computes the cash payout value of unused earned or privilege leave when an employee opts to encash it rather than take it as time off. In India, this is governed by Section 10(10AA) of the Income Tax Act, which provides specific tax exemptions on leave encashment received at the time of resignation or retirement.

This calculator determines your encashment amount using the standard formula of (Monthly Basic Salary ÷ Days in Month) × Days to Encash. It shows your daily rate, the total gross payout, and your remaining leave balance — giving both employees and HR teams the clarity to plan leave policy decisions and financial payouts accurately.

Why Should You Use the Leave Encashment Calculator?

  • Calculate your payout value: Know exactly how much cash you will receive for unused leave days before accepting a resignation or retirement package — preventing surprises at settlement.
  • Verify HR calculations: Cross-check the encashment figure shown in your full and final settlement statement against the standard formula — a common source of discrepancies in payroll departments.
  • Plan leave strategy: If you have a large leave balance, knowing the encashment value helps you decide whether it is more financially beneficial to take the leave or encash it.
  • HR leave liability management: HR teams can use this calculator to estimate the total leave encashment liability on the books — essential for provisions in financial statements.
  • Different divisors supported: Some jurisdictions and employers use 26 days (working days) rather than 30 days as the monthly divisor — this calculator lets you set either value.
  • Instant balance visibility: The remaining leave balance field shows how many days remain after the encashment — useful for employees who are partially encashing their balance.

How to Use the Leave Encashment Calculator

  1. Select your currency — formats all monetary output.
  2. Enter Monthly Basic Salary — your gross basic salary per month (exclude HRA, allowances, and variable pay; use basic only for standard encashment calculations).
  3. Enter Standard Days in Month — typically 26 (working days under Indian labour law) or 30 (calendar days). Your employment contract or HR policy will specify which to use.
  4. Enter Total Unused Leave Balance — the total number of earned/privilege leave days you have accumulated and not yet taken.
  5. Enter Days to Encash — the number of days you want to convert to cash (cannot exceed your total balance).
  6. Click Calculate Payout to see your daily rate, gross encashment value, and remaining leave balance.

How Leave Encashment is Calculated

Daily Rate = Monthly Basic Salary ÷ Standard Days in Month
Gross Encashment Value = Daily Rate × Days to Encash
Remaining Balance = Total Leave Balance − Days to Encash

Worked Example

Monthly basic salary: ₹40,000. Days in month: 26. Total leave balance: 30 days. Days to encash: 10 days.

  • Daily Rate = ₹40,000 ÷ 26 = ₹1,538.46/day
  • Gross Encashment = ₹1,538.46 × 10 = ₹15,384.60
  • Remaining Balance = 30 − 10 = 20 days

Leave Encashment — Key Things to Know

gavel

26 vs 30 Days Divisor

In India, most private sector employers use 26 working days as the monthly divisor (excluding Sundays). Some use 30 calendar days. This difference affects the daily rate significantly — always check your employment contract.

currency_rupee

Tax Exemption at Separation

When you leave a job or retire, leave encashment is exempt under Sec 10(10AA) up to ₹25 lakh (Budget 2023). Government employees get full exemption with no limit. Private sector employees use the "least of 4" limit calculation.

warning

Taxable During Employment

Leave encashment received while still employed is treated as salary income and fully taxable in the same financial year. This applies to mid-year or annual encashment allowances offered by employers.

calculate

Encashment Limit Caps

Many employers cap the number of leave days that can be encashed — e.g., a maximum of 30 days per year or a lifetime maximum. Check your HR policy document before relying on a balance figure from the HRIS.

account_balance

Leave Liability for HR

From an accounting perspective, accumulated leave represents a financial liability on the employer's balance sheet. HR and Finance teams should review the total leave encashment liability quarterly to ensure adequate provisions.

info

Privilege vs Casual Leave

In India, earned/privilege leave (EL/PL) is typically encashable. Casual leave (CL) and sick leave (SL) generally are not. Always check your company's leave policy to confirm which leave types are eligible for encashment.
