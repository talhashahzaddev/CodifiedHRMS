Why Use Free HR Calculators?

Human Resources teams rely on dozens of recurring calculations — from daily time card math to quarterly attrition reviews. Spreadsheets work, but they're fragile: formulas break, files get misplaced, and version control becomes a nightmare. These free browser-based tools eliminate that friction entirely.

Every calculator on this page implements the industry-standard formula for that metric. The Absence Percentage Calculator uses the universally accepted formula: (Absent ÷ (Employees × Workdays)) × 100. The Attrition Rate Calculator follows SHRM's recommended methodology. The Leave Encashment Calculator applies the exact Indian Income Tax Act, Section 10(10AA) exemption logic.

Key Workforce Metrics Explained

person_off

Absenteeism Rate

Measures what percentage of total scheduled work time is lost to absences. Industry benchmark: under 3% is excellent; above 5% signals a systemic issue.

(Absent Days ÷ (Employees × Workdays)) × 100
trending_down

Attrition Rate

Measures the rate at which employees leave the organisation. Industry average (US, 2024) is ~23% annually. Below 10% is considered very low attrition.

(Employees Left ÷ Avg Headcount) × 100
groups

Full-Time Equivalent (FTE)

Converts all hours worked (including part-time) into full-time unit equivalents, enabling accurate headcount budgeting and ACA compliance reporting.

Total Hours Worked ÷ Standard Full-Time Hours
